## Screenshots

Neither store accepts a listing without them, and I cannot produce them from
here — they must come from a real device or simulator.

**Required:** iPhone 6.7" (1290×2796) and 6.5"; Android phone screenshots at a
16:9 or taller ratio. iPad shots only if you keep `supportsTablet: true` in
`app.config.ts` — Apple will ask for them if the app declares iPad support, so
either capture them or set that to `false`.

**Suggested order**, leading with what is distinctive rather than a sign-in
screen: Prayers (with times and reminder state) · Focus (mid-session) · Garden
(several grown trees) · Dashboard · Adhkar · Study room.
